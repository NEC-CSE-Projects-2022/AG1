Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma (OSCC) Dataset

This dataset consists of histopathological images used for the classification of Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma (OSCC). It supports deep learning based medical image analysis including segmentation and classification tasks.Dataset comprises 1,224 histopathological images utilized for the classification of Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma (OSCC). The images are collected at two different Magnification levels. At 100x Magnification, there are 89 images of normal oral Epithelium and 439 images of OSCC tissue. At 400x Magnification, the dataset includes 201 images of normal Epithelium and 495 images of OSCC. These images offer a diverse representation of both healthy and cancerous tissue, aiding in effective model training and evaluation for automated OSCC detection
