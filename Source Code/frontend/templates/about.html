About XSFN-Net

Problem Statement

  • OSCC remains one of the most prevalent and aggressive forms of oral cancer worldwide.
  • Traditional diagnosis via manual microscopy is labor-intensive and prone to subjectivity.
  • Existing AI-based models often require large computational power and lack explainability.
  • XSFN-Net addresses these limitations through fractional spiking neurons and hybrid feature learning.

Methodology Overview

  • Preprocessing: Median adaptive filter (MedAV) for noise removal and tissue preservation.
  • Segmentation: PraNet-based lesion extraction focusing on epithelial regions.
  • Feature Extraction: Dual pipeline capturing handcrafted (DWT, LVP, GLRM) and deep CNN features.
  • Feature Fusion: Fractional calculus integration enhances discrimination and stability.
  • Classification: Fully connected spiking layer predicts Normal vs OSCC with adaptive thresholding.

Key Contributions & Achievements

🚀 Innovative Architecture

Introduced a hybrid deep spiking-fractional framework to enhance OSCC classification performance.

⚙️ Lightweight Computation

Fractional derivatives and model pruning reduce training complexity, enabling edge deployment.

📊 Superior Accuracy

Achieved an overall accuracy of 91.6% with excellent sensitivity and precision across datasets.

🧠 Explainable Decisions

Integrates interpretability maps aligning with pathologists’ diagnostic observations.
